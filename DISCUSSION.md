# Discussion register: things the model cannot or should not carry

Murray, 2026-10-28: some things cannot be modelled, or only with very sketchy figures. They may not belong on the dashboard, but they belong in the papers (discussion, limitations, further work). This file is their record. **Rule: the moment something is found to be unmodellable, too sketchy to model or out of the model's scope, add a row here with what we do know**, even if a work row in SCOPE.md also exists. Each row says what it is, why it is not in the model, the best figures and how sketchy they are, and where it should be discussed. Papers refer to PUBLICATIONS.md.

Quality: **firm** (a published figure we can cite), **indicative** (a figure with a clear source but a loose link to the model), **sketchy** (an order of magnitude or an analogy), **none** (no figure found).
