Subject: Rebuilding the HALF index for Australia: two inputs we may have misread

Dear [name of corresponding author],

We compare methods for how many people Australia's land can feed, and we rebuilt the HALF index from Alexander et al. 2016 (Global Environmental Change 41: 88-98) using the paper and its supplementary information. We had no access to your 2015 food balance download, so we used the current FAOSTAT revision, with 70 of the 90 commodities.

For Australia in 2011 your index is 112.2. Ours is 113.12 on current FAOSTAT (+0.8%). When we put in the world pasture area the supplement prints (3,364 Mha, against 3,182 Mha in the current revision), it is 118.88 (+6.0%). With every input the paper and supplement print, it is 121.59 (+8.4%). The same inputs move our world, United States, Argentina and India figures closer to yours, so the 0.8% holds on the current revision only.

Two questions, if you are willing:

1. Is 3,364 Mha the pasture area behind the Australian figure, and which FAOSTAT definition and year does it come from?
2. Your supplement gives bovine meat 1,982 Mha of that pasture (59%). We share pasture by feed need and get 67%. Is the share set another way?

We would correct our reconstruction and credit your reply.
