# Draft email to the Cassidy authors (not sent; for Murray)

To: [to: corresponding author, from the paper]
Subject: Rebuilding people nourished per hectare: where we may have misread the recipe

Dear [name of corresponding author],

We compare methods for how many people a country's land can feed, and we rebuilt the people-per-hectare measure of Cassidy et al. 2013 (Environmental Research Letters 8: 034015) from the paper alone. We did not have the supplementary information. We used 22 of your 41 crops, FAOSTAT 2011 in place of your 1997 to 2003 data, and assumed the crop energy per kilogram and the grain-fed share of beef.

As run, our figures are 22% to 42% above yours: world 12.3 against 10.1 people a hectare produced (+22.0%), and delivered 24.9% to 42.3% above across the world and four countries. With each crop at its 1997 to 2003 yield the gaps fall to +5% to +25%. The United States other-use share is 32% in our run against 6% in the paper, and the feed share is 41% against 67%.

Two questions, if you are willing:

1. Which crop energy values and grain-fed share of beef did you use, and are they in the supplementary information?
2. Does your "other" use include biofuels and industrial use only, or also seed and losses? Our split puts seed and losses outside it, and we suspect that is our misreading.

We would correct our reconstruction and credit your reply.

Yours sincerely,
[name, affiliation]
