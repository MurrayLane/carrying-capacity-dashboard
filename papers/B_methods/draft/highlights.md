# Highlights

- Published capacity estimates run from about 9 to 100 million, both ends soft
- If productivity is an input, footprint and HALF close most of their gap to the model
- Footprint and HALF at Australia's own productivity read 1.6 to 2.8 times the model
- The pasture-crop pairing is one of several reasons the model reads below HALF
- One at a time, weather gives 18.9 million (40.4 on crops only), no waste 55.3
