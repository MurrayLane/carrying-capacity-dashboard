# Ecological Economics: submission requirements that bind Paper B (from the guide for authors, read by Murray 2026-11-14; summarised, not copied)

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/ecological-economics/publish/guide-for-authors (CiteScore 12.4; impact factor 6.7 as shown on the page that day).

## Article type and length
* Two types fit: "Methodological and Ideological Options" (develops methodologies or investigates the implications of assumptions; reviewed on originality and usefulness; maximum 8,000 words or 25 double-spaced pages) and "Analysis" (research articles on important questions; reviewed on originality, quality, accuracy and importance; maximum 8,000 words). "Surveys" (reviews of subject areas, 8,000 words) is the third possibility if the paper is read as a review.
* Main text under 8,000 words (the guide does not say whether references count; keep the main text under 8,000 and the references outside it).
* Abstract no more than 250 words; stands alone; purpose, procedures, main findings, principal conclusions; no references unless essential (then author and year in full).
* 1 to 7 keywords, single words preferred.
* Highlights required: 3 to 5 bullet points, each at most 85 characters including spaces, in a separate file with "highlights" in its name.
* Graphical abstract encouraged, not required (at least 1328 pixels wide; TIFF, EPS, PDF or Office).

## Structure and style
* Numbered sections and subsections (1, 1.1, 1.1.1); cross-references by number; the abstract outside the numbering.
* Tables as editable text with captions, cited in the text, numbered in order of appearance, notes below the table, no vertical rules; not duplicating what the text says.
* Figures as separate files, cited and numbered in order, each with a caption (brief title plus description); vector as EPS or PDF; bitmaps at least 300 dpi (1063 pixels single column, 2244 full page); line drawings 1000 dpi; no combined multi-panel files except for direct comparisons; colour must be readable with impaired colour vision.
* Maps: show only the study area; add the note that map lines delineate study areas and do not necessarily depict accepted national boundaries.
* Appendices lettered A, B; their equations, tables and figures numbered A.1, B.1.
* Supplementary material submitted with the manuscript, each file cited in the text with a caption; published as received.
* File format: Word (.docx) single column, or LaTeX; PDF not accepted as source.

## Declarations and statements (all required at submission)
* Title page: title without abbreviations; author names; affiliation with full postal address; corresponding author with email (published).
* Declaration of competing interests via the declarations tool (the model under test is the author's own: declare it).
* Funding statement; if none: "This research did not receive any specific grant from funding agencies in the public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors."
* Declaration of generative AI use: a section before the reference list, in the journal's form: "During the preparation of this work the author(s) used [tool] to [reason]. After using this tool/service, the author(s) reviewed and edited the content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the content of the published article." The author is accountable for all content, including checking every reference.
* CRediT author statement (single author: all roles that apply).
* Research data: Option C applies. Deposit the research data in a repository and give the link at submission, or state why sharing is not possible; the statement is published. The archive is private and the Zenodo deposit is on hold (decision needed before submission).
* Acknowledgements as a separate section before the references.
* Co-submission to MethodsX or Data in Brief is offered (article charge applies) but not required.

## References
* Any consistent style at submission; the journal's style is applied at proof. Author and year in text: one author "(Allan, 2020)"; two "(Allan and Jones, 2019)"; three or more "(Kramer et al., 2023)"; same author and year distinguished a, b.
* Every reference must be real, complete (authors, title, journal or book, year, volume, pages or article number) and carry a DOI where one exists. References cited in the abstract given in full.
* List alphabetical then chronological; journal names abbreviated by the List of Title Word Abbreviations.
* Datasets and software cited with [dataset] or [software], version and persistent identifier.
* Web references with full URL and date accessed.
* Preprints marked as such; unpublished work and personal communications best kept out of the list.

## Process
* Single-anonymised review by at least two reviewers after editorial assessment; one appeal allowed.
* Preprint on SSRN offered at submission, free; posting does not affect the decision.
* Proof corrections within two days; AI-generated alt text to be checked at proof.
* Transfer to another Elsevier journal may be offered on rejection.
