## 2. Generative AI declaration, fuller version (for a cover letter or supplement)

> **Tools.** I used Claude models made by Anthropic (Opus and Sonnet, with a coordinating session on a further Claude model [CONFIRM name and version]), run as agents in the Claude Code environment. Each job was a written brief with named inputs and named outputs, and each returned a written report. The briefs and reports are kept in the project repository.
>
> **What the tools did.** Under my direction they rebuilt the model and ran its analyses; retrieved sources and read them at page level; and drafted text from my outline, the section plans and the registers, citing only register rows. Three registers hold the project's facts: sources, literature and claims, with one row for every dataset, cited work and number that may enter a paper.
>
> **How the work was checked.** Every headline number has two independent routes: the builder's script, and a second model session that derived the value on its own from the data and the documents, stating its tolerance first and planting a wrong value to show the comparison can fail. Builders and checkers were different models. Drafts were checked sentence by sentence against the registers by a session that had not seen the drafting notes. Separate sessions wrote simulated referee reports, which I used to find weaknesses to fix. These are not real reviews.
>
> **What I did.** I set the question, the scope, the methods, the journals and every decision, each recorded in the decisions file in my words. I fetched the works the tools could not reach. I approved each verified result at recorded gates, on a digest of plain statements, before anything rested on it. I reviewed and edited all content, I checked the reference lists, and I take full responsibility for it.
>
> After using these tools, I reviewed and edited the content as needed and take full responsibility for the content of the published article.
