# Publication strategy

Status: saved 2026-10-25. Papers come out of the results files the scope produces; none
needs work outside SCOPE.md once Workstreams E and F are in it. Order matters: papers 1,
2 and 4 use the data refresh with the method frozen (SCOPE D1); paper 3 uses the method
changes compared one at a time against that frozen baseline.

Prior work this builds on, all with 2005 to 2011 data and the fixed 60-zone geography:
Lane 2010 Land Use Policy (methodology review); Lane 2011 Urban Design (planning
argument); Lane and Dawes 2013 Urban Environment (regional case studies); Lane, Dawes
and Grace 2014 Ecological Modelling (essential parameters); Lane 2014 thesis (full
method, the reference for everything here); Lane, Dawes and Grace 2015 Population and
Environment (scale); the time-frames paper (ePrints 99790); Lane 2015 IAFOR (policy).

## Paper 1. Australia's carrying capacity, 2011 to 2026

Question: same model, same 60 zones, data brought forward 15 years; which regions moved
between under and over capacity, by how much, and how much of the change is population,
land use or yield.
Why first: method frozen, like-for-like, and every later paper cites its baseline.
Uses: A1 baseline (old data, old zones); D2 zones; D3 result sets; D4 decomposition.
Venue: Population and Environment (sequel) or Land Use Policy.

## Paper 2. How carrying capacity falls as the unit of self-sufficiency shrinks

Question: the 2015 paper had three points (national 40 million, states 28 million,
regions 23 million); give the whole curve, national to SA3, on current data, and separate
the boundary effect (landscape regions versus 2011 NRM zones on identical data) from the
scale effect. Where does the curve bend and why: excess pasture, or cities without
hinterland.
Uses: B0, B0a, B0b, B0c; D3.
Venue: Population and Environment, answering its own 2015 article.

## Paper 3. The essential parameters revisited

Question: which of the 2014 paper's assumptions carried and which did not. Each change
tested alone against the frozen baseline and reported as its effect on national and
regional capacity: spatial pasture productivity; a water ceiling on irrigation; a
non-stationary climate series (absorbs the time-frames sequel); Australian loss factors;
the organic yield penalty; an energy-to-yield link.
Framing: lead with affordability, supply chains and climate as the energy constraint,
not the 2007 to 2013 peak-oil literature; keep every energy parameter.
Uses: Workstream E, all rows.
Venue: Ecological Modelling.

## Paper 4. Populations scaled to place, a decade on

Question: four or five regions in depth chosen from paper 1's largest movers (a capital
with hinterland, a Murray-Darling Basin region, south-west WA, Tasmania, an arid region);
the 2015 migration map redrawn.
Uses: paper 1 results only.
Venue: Australian Planner or Regional Studies, Regional Science.

## Supporting paper. The model and data as a citable resource

The open workbook, the SA2 data layer, grouping files and browser evaluator, archived
with a DOI. Holds the reproducibility material so papers 1 to 4 stay short.
Uses: Workstream F, all rows.
Venue: Environmental Modelling and Software or Scientific Data.

## Not written separately

A sequel to the 2010 methodology review; its material is paper 3's introduction.

## Order

Supporting paper's archive first (it is cited by all); paper 1; paper 2; paper 4 can run
alongside 2; paper 3 after Workstream E.
