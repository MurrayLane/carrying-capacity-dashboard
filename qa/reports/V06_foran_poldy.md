# V06 verification report: commodity balance (Foran and Poldy 2002), independent rebuild (Opus checker, blind), 2026-11-07

**Agreement with the project's test (`foran_poldy_balance.py`).** On 2021-23 production the checker gets grain direct 486 million, grain with embodied feed grain 128, meat 40.4, fish 16.9; the project's figures are 486, 128, 40 and 17. Per-person requirements (base case): grain 108.1 kg, feed grain 302.8, meat 115.3, fish 17.5 (project: 108, 303, 115, 17). The 1997-98 FAO check: grain 31.95 Mt mean against their 31.578 (1.2% apart), meat 3.47 against 3.514 (1.2%). **Holds, within 1% on every figure.**

**Findings about the source.** (1) The report's text claims a positive balance for every item except fish, but in its own Table 2.5 the 0.67% scenario's meat requirement (3.804 Mt) exceeds 1997-98 production (3.514), which the text covers only by saying animal numbers could rise. (2) Its "25 to 45% of current grain production" fits a divisor of about 30 Mt, not the 31.578 Mt it prints (25.8% to 43.0% on the printed figure). (3) The per-person requirements differ between scenarios (grain 103.7, 108.1, 109.8 kg) in a ratio that does not match the population ratio, so the Table 2.5 populations or the diet differ from Table 1.1; dividing by the zero scenario would raise the people-fed figures about 4%. (4) The embodied feed grain is a fixed 2.63 kg per kg of meat.

**Findings about the data.** FAO's Australian fish production, imports and food use are identical in 2021, 2022 and 2023 (295, 593 and 641 kt): a carried-forward value, not a three-year mean. 2023 cereals (43.1 Mt) is a low year that pulls the mean down. The weight basis of the report's meat and fish (carcase or retail; live or edible) is not stated.

**Status.** The Foran and Poldy claims (register rows for the meat, grain and fish readings and the per-person requirements) are independently verified. The source findings go to the paper's section on the method and to the literature register notes.
