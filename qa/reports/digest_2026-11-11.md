# Approval digest, 2026-11-11: the rebuilt decomposition of the 2011 to 2025 change (gate 3)

Twelve new Tier A claims (A354 to A365). Each was computed by the builder's script (B76) and reproduced exactly by an independent script written without sight of the builder's (V29), in all 120 cells (60 zones, two presets). The two Tier A totals they decompose (A072, +8,195,723 short term; A073, +273,063 long term) were approved at gate 2 and are unchanged. Nothing else in the papers moves. All figures are national, on the corrected releases (2011-fixed to 2025-fixed), and are counted in people of capacity.

Read each statement; reply "approved", or name the number of any statement you query.

1. Between 2011 and 2025 the corrected national short-term capacity rose from 33.31 million to 41.50 million people. Walking the data forward one layer at a time, the layers that moved it were: livestock (+4.70 million), consumption (+4.18 million), the red-meat mix of the diet (+1.81 million), crop yields (+0.12 million), and land (-2.62 million). The other layers moved it by nothing in the short term. (A354, A356 to A358, A362)

2. The corrected national long-term capacity rose from 15.35 million to 15.62 million. The layers were: land (-1.21 million), livestock (+0.60 million), the red-meat mix (+0.40 million), crop yields (+0.22 million), the organic update (+0.17 million), long-term liquid fuel (+0.09 million), and the resilience table (-7 people). (A354, A356, A357, A359 to A362)

3. In the long term, land is the largest single layer and it is negative. The old table, which stopped one layer short and was built on the published method, had crop yields largest; that reading is withdrawn. (A365)

4. The layers add up exactly to the 2011 to 2025 change, in every zone and both presets, with no interaction term, because each layer is measured after the ones before it. The order of the walk is the order of the data releases; a different order would share the change out differently but sum the same. (A354 to A362; V29)

5. The infrastructure-land layer (land that follows population) moves national capacity by nothing; it matters in one region only (Northern and Yorke). (A355)

6. The short-term split between the consumption layer and the organic layer is a bookkeeping choice: the table keeps the organic share at its 2011 value through the consumption step. On the strict chain the two layers read +3.80 million and +0.38 million instead of +4.18 million and 0; the sum is the same. The README states both. (A358, A359)

7. Population is reported as a change of status, not as a cause. In 2011 Australia's people were 0.670 of short-term capacity and 1.454 of long-term capacity. In 2025 they are 0.665 of short-term capacity and 1.767 of long-term capacity. Above 1 means more people than the long-term capacity. (A363, A364)

8. These rows are the basis of Paper A section 4. Until approved, no draft or table rests on them; after approval their register status becomes "Murray-verified (gate 3)".
