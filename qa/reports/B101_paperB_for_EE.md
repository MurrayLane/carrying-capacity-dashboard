## Wrong-value check

Wrong-value check: EXAMPLE ONLY, not something done. Leading with the method claim invites the sentence "inputs explain only 10% of the published spread". It would change B276's claim three ways: B276 measures each method reading's distance from the corrected model on a ratio scale, not the spread of published estimates; its 10% holds for one set of rows with the methods weighted equally and runs to 18% on others; and the published spread (9.4 to 100 million, B005) mixes definitions, data years and the author's own pre-correction figure, which the harmonised comparison never ran. The text written here keeps B276's base ("10% of the distance between the methods and the corrected model ... 10 to 18% with the set of rows chosen") and says "only in small part" where no base is stated. The check that catches the wrong form: read the claim's statement and notes for what the percentage is a share of, and confirm the sentence names the same denominator.
