# Open-access fetch, 2026-11-10 (coordinator)

Murray asked whether the open-access papers in `acquisitions_2026-11-09.md` section D could be fetched directly. Result: 23 of the 47 not already held were filed in the archive (`carrying-capacity-models/Literature review/`); 28 remain for Murray to fetch by hand (`acquisitions_2026-11-10.md` section D).

## What was done
1. For each DOI, the Unpaywall index was asked for its open-access locations; each location was downloaded with a plain client string and kept only if the bytes were a PDF. Publisher PDF patterns (Nature, IOP, MDPI, Springer, Frontiers, Wiley, Science, Annual Reviews) were tried after that. 14 PDFs came through this way. Each was opened and its first page checked against the title (all 14 match; Bentham 2020 and Bolleter 2018 are repository or author-manuscript copies).
2. For the rest, Europe PMC's search API was asked for an open PubMed Central record; where one exists, the full text (JATS XML) was taken from Europe PMC's REST service and rendered to a text file alongside the XML. 9 papers came through this way. These are full texts, not publisher PDFs; the register says so in `notes`.
3. A headless-browser pass was refused by the session's safety check as bot-check circumvention and was not attempted again. The 28 remaining papers sit with publishers that refuse automated download (Elsevier, MDPI, Wiley, Science, Annual Reviews, Taylor & Francis) or returned a web page instead of the file (Nature, IOP); they need a person in a browser.
