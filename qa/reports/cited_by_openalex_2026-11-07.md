## Reading (coordinator)
Across the four works there are about 60 distinct citing works. Almost all are the Chinese "resource and environment carrying capacity" literature (urban, water, ecological and land carrying capacity, evaluated with index systems), plus a few in Iran, Indonesia and Croatia; the Australian citations are Bolleter's northern-Australia urbanisation work (2018, twice) and a 2025 environmental-education paper. None appears to be a food-and-land capacity estimate for Australia or a critique of the model's method. The uptake of the model has therefore been as a cited precedent in a different literature, not as a method applied or contested, which is itself a statement the review can make. Whether any of the 60 engages with the method rather than citing it in passing needs reading: a Sonnet job to open each abstract and classify (applies the method; discusses it; cites in passing) would settle it, about half a point of the allowance.
