## How to read this

Every number and statement in the two papers is a row (a claim) in a register. Paper A is the results paper and paper B the methods paper. Each claim has a tier: A is a headline result or anything a reader would quote and needs two independent routes to agree; B is a supporting number needing one independent check; C is context. The status says how far the claim has been checked, from unverified through self-checked to independently verified. The producing script is route one; a verifier report in `qa/reports/` that re-derived the claim blind is route two. Section 2 lists each Tier A claim on one line; the last column says what is still open (a status short of independently verified, or a defect that names the row or its output file). Section 3 lists the Tier A claims that lack a blind report or are not plain independently verified, which is what gate 2 cannot pass with. Section 4 lists every defect not yet fixed. Section 5 is a fixed sample of Tier B rows. You read every Tier A line, sample Tier B, and are not asked to re-derive.
