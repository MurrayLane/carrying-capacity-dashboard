4. **The 6.1% in "5.8 to 6.1%".** The derivation here gives 5.8% only; Paper B's upper figure rests on its method comparison. The coordinator should reconcile the two papers.
5. **Flag name.** New rows use "[gate 5]" (decision 114 lapses the gate 4 digest); older rows keep "[gate 4]". One rename pass would settle it.
