# V02 verification report: independent rebuild of the HALF index (Opus checker, blind to the project's implementation), 2026-11-07

The checker rebuilt the index from the paper and its supplement, on the same FAOSTAT extracts, without opening the project's script, README, register or papers. Its code: scratchpad v02/half_v02.py.

**Its values (HALF, % of world land).** The treatment of animal fats is the largest open choice in the paper. Headline reading (raw fats take a share of the meat animals' land by energy): world 2011 31.4, Australia 2011 96.0, Australia 2021-23 68.7 (own system) or 74.1 (2011 production system, the paper's diet-only convention). No-fat-land reading (only butter and cream get land, from milk): world 34.1, Australia 2011 112.4, Australia 2021-23 79.8 (own system) or 86.1 (2011 system). The paper: world 35.13, Australia 112.20. The no-fat-land reading matches the paper (+0.2% for Australia, -3% for the world) and fits the paper's own animal-food area (3,700 of about 3,868 Mha), so it is the reading the paper most likely used.

**Against the project's implementation (half_index.py: world 34.4, Australia 2011 120.9, Australia 2021-23 84.2).** The two implementations differ by 8% on Australia 2011 and 5% on 2021-23 under the closest reading. The checker's diagnostics point to the cause on the project's side: its 2011 feed pool is 293 Mt of dry matter against the checker's 1,106 and the paper's 1,515, and its allocation gives monogastric animals feed cropland in proportion to need over pool, which with a pool smaller than need allocates more than the feed cropland exists (about four times). This inflates pork, poultry and egg land and so Australia's value. Confirmed by reading lines 72 to 78 of half_index.py. Defect X036.

**Other findings.** Both implementations differ from the paper on the ruminant split (checker 2,464 Mt total ruminant feed need against the SI's 2,809; beef gets 67% of pasture against the paper's 58.9%; offals at a ratio of 15 would roughly close it). The paper's own totals do not add (871 + 3,700 is given as 4,484, and 35.1% of 13,009 is 4,566; a typo), and the SI has two small slips (soy oil area 52.9 then 52.5; a formula labelled feed area computes food area). The new FAOSTAT balance has no oilseed-cake or molasses items, holds oil palm fruit under "Palm kernels", and flips the stock-variation sign relative to the paper's data; the balance csv repeats three item rows.

**Status.** The HALF claims (register rows for the fidelity table, Australia 2021-23 = 84.2 and the 265 million reading) are not verified and are expected to change: the project implementation needs the feed-pool fault fixed and the fat treatment stated, then the two implementations reconciled to a stated tolerance.
