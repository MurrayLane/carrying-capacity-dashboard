## Wrong-value check

*Example, marked as such.* Section 7 point 6 reads "a third to two fifths (33% in 2011, 38% in 2025)". A fix that would itself be wrong: replacing "about a third" with section 5's figures, "23% in 2025", to make the two sections agree. A090's 23% is the NRM sum on the state-yield basis, while A145 and A148 are on each zone's own yields; printing 23% in section 7 would set a state-yield share beside the own-yield regional capacities that section reports, the very mixing of bases that V32a problem 2 asks the paper to stop. The fix is to name both bases, not to make one number serve both.
