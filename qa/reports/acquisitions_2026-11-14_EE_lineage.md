# Acquisitions: the Ecological Economics footprint-debate works for Paper B's lineage (B105)

Upload link: https://github.com/MurrayLane/carrying-capacity-models/upload/main/Literature%20review

## Recommendation: what to fetch

Fetch two files.

1. Wackernagel et al. 1999, Ecological Economics 29(3), 375-390. It is the national accounting paper, so it carries per-nation footprint and capacity figures. If Paper B quotes any of them, it must be read.
2. van den Bergh and Rietveld 2004, BioScience 54(3). It is a meta-analysis of published world carrying-capacity estimates, so it carries a spread of estimates and a central value. If Paper B quotes the range or the centre, it must be read.

Fetch Wackernagel and Rees 1997 only if Paper B quotes one of its worked per-capita land figures. If Paper B cites it only as the origin of the footprint, no file is needed.

Every other work below is cited for lineage, for a critique, or for a reply, with no number quoted. Under the "as reported" rule each can be cited from its Crossref record with no file.

Two cautions from the Crossref check:
- The five 2000 forum pieces in volume 32(3) are all recorded on Crossref under the bare title "Discussion", with no author names. The DOIs and page ranges below are real, but Crossref cannot confirm which page range is which author. That match must be checked on the publisher page before the register row is written. The title text for the Wackernagel and Silverstein reply and the Rees reply is from memory of the forum, not from Crossref.
- "Kitzes et al. 2009 (Ecological Economics)" fits two records on Crossref. Both are listed (works 9a and 9b). Work 9a answers Fiala, so it pairs with item 8; work 9b is the larger research agenda.
