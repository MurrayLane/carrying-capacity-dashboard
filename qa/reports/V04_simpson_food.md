# V04 verification report: food row of the ecological footprint (Simpson et al. 2000), independent rebuild (Opus checker, blind), 2026-11-07

**Against the paper, 1991-92 (ha a person).** Checker: plant-food crop 0.142, animal feed crop 0.094, pasture 2.385; paper 0.312, 0.095, 1.758. Project (`simpson_food.py`): 0.107, 0.098, 1.737. The feed crop reproduces in both builds; the plant-food crop reproduces in neither (both under half the paper's, and the checker shows apparent consumption at world yields cannot reach 0.312; candidate explanations it could not test: Australian rather than world yields, total cropland rather than harvested area, production-based rather than consumption-based figures, or figures carried from Wackernagel's earlier work). Pasture: the project matches the paper (1.2% low) and the checker is 36% high; the difference is the rule for sharing world pasture among beef, sheep and goat meat and milk, which the paper does not state (project: by edible protein; checker: by production times feed conversion ratio). The paper's own text (60% of pasture for meat) and table (67%) also disagree.

**Current data, 2021-23 (ha a person).** Checker: crop 0.227, pasture 1.082; project: crop 0.198, pasture 0.860. Capacity readings: checker 140 million on cropland (binding) and 307 on pasture; project 160 on cropland and 343 together. The two builds agree on the direction and size of the change since 1991-92 (pasture per person roughly halves, from falling beef consumption and rising world output per hectare; cropland per person barely moves) and disagree by 15% on cropland and 26% on pasture, from the sharing rule and the item mapping (oil area charged wholly to oil by the checker; ABS product weights mapped to primary crops by stated factors).

**Verdict.** The method is under-specified in the paper: a second implementation from the same description differs from the first by 15% to 36% on the land figures, and neither reaches the paper's plant-food figure. The Simpson row cannot be reported as a single reconstruction; it should carry the range across the two readings, the pasture-rule dependence, and the unreproduced plant-food figure. Defects X041, X042.
