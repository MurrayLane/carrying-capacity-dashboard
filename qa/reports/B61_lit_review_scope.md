**On hand, but the text does not support the use the register or earlier reports give:**

* L079 Ridoutt 2017: a tonnes-against-tonnes balance with an index, no people count of its own (B54a).
* L076 Bryan 2016 and L135 Grundy 2016: no people count; context for land competition and yield trajectories, not comparators (B50a, B52).
* L081 Willett (EAT-Lancet): a judged reference diet, not an optimisation; diet change alone moves global cropland only 0 to 2%, so it does not carry "diet moves a land-based number" (B50b).
* L088 Chapman and Byron: excludes human carrying capacity by design; cite for the ecological lineage only, and cite Sayre for the static-versus-variable line it quotes (B54a).
* L129 Wackernagel 2019: not a reply to the critics; the defence is L086 Goldfinger (B54b).
* L109 Wang 2022: single author; the 4 to 157 billion range on p. 4 is second-hand (B54b).
* L124 Shepon 2016: extra people fed from a reallocation, a gain, not a capacity (B48).
* L101 Peters 2008: a commentary with no method; it announces the 2009 paper (B50b).
* L103 Zumkehr and Campbell: the 90% is the 100-mile case and the result turns on radius, not diet (B54a). L104 Kurtz: 351 to 481 km, and the shares are of the metropolitan population (B54a).
* L114 Schon and Bohringer: a German state and five sub-regions; no population count (B54b).
* L117 van Zanten: "below 1" holds for protein only (B54b). L119 Clark and Tilman: land occupied, not land available (B48).
* L111, L113, L120: figures need their base year or scope (2005 base; 2014 cereals; farm-gate emissions excluding land clearing) (B50b).
* L095 Hadjikakou: do not quote its "land use" share (B20).
* L091 Graymore: a regional pressure index; it says nothing about local food production, so the pairing in the L073 note should not be carried forward (B50a).
* L075 Hatfield-Dodds and L090: scenario outlooks, context only.

## Gaps in brief

Paper B's catalogue (section 4) is well supplied: of about 55 likely citations, about 40 have been read in full, and the gaps are founding works the project holds only second-hand or in translation (Gifford et al. 1975, the English Fearnside 1985, the stocks-and-flows method paper) and a nutrition-optimisation family with no representative work on hand. Its concept section (2) rests on classic papers that are in the archive and were read for the thesis but not in this project, so it needs a reading pass more than acquisitions, plus a few post-2010 voices and planning uses. Section 3 has no review-method literature at all. Paper A is thinner: its introduction lacks an academic account of the 2011 to 2025 population debate, of food security in Australian planning, and of observed drought and climate effects on Australian farming. Its discussion of measures is well supplied for diet change only; livestock productivity, Australian food waste, grain storage, drought-proofing, trade between regions and imports have little or nothing on hand. Its limitations lack sources on self-sufficiency as a policy concept, urban agriculture, water and fuel. The 2010 review is missing from the archive, about twenty read works were not kept, and eleven or so on-hand sources carry register uses their text does not support.
