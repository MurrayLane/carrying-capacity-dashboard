## L229 House of Representatives inquiry, Australian Food Story (2023), serves A1.4

PDF has 227 pages; printed page = PDF page minus 26 for the body. There is no executive summary; the Foreword (pp. iii-v) plays that part, followed by the list of 35 recommendations (pp. xix-xxv).
- Headline finding: Australia is "a food secure nation. It produces far more than it consumes ... and that is likely to remain the case for decades to come" (para 3.125, p. 54), with pockets of food insecurity inside it (3.125; Chapter 7).
- Key recommendation: a National Food Plan with measurable targets (Rec. 1, p. xix), a Minister for Food in Prime Minister and Cabinet (Rec. 2) and a National Food Council (Rec. 3). Others: dairy strategy (Rec. 4), seafood strategy (Rec. 5), protect farmland from urban sprawl (Rec. 13, p. xxi), national food supply chain map (Rec. 14), food waste measures (Rec. 18, p. xxii), household food insecurity survey every 3 years (Rec. 29, p. xxiv).
- Production against consumption and exports: ABARES 2020 says about 70% of agricultural production is exported and imports are about 11% of food consumption by value (3.2, p. 23). ABARES 2018: 1988-89 to 2016-17 food exports rose from 30 to 33% of indicative production, imports from 8 to 15% of consumption (3.15-3.16, pp. 26-27). Grains 2021-22: a record 67.4 Mt crop, wheat 36.3 Mt (3.48, p. 34). Dairy 2021-22: 8.5 billion litres, lowest since at least 1996-97 (3.20, p. 28).
- Submitter claims, not committee findings: "enough food to feed 75 million people" (National Farmers' Federation, 3.8, p. 24; repeated 3.11, p. 25); "26 million people at home and about 50 million overseas" (a university submission, p. 25).
- Not found: any national self-sufficiency percentage or target. "Self-sufficiency" appears for dairy (p. 29) and chicken meat (p. 31) only.
- Also taken (for A11.3): 7.6 Mt lost or wasted a year, $36.6 billion, 3.5% of emissions; by stage 22% primary, 17% processing, 3% distribution, 7% wholesale or retail, 32% households, 16% hospitality, 3% institutions (5.64, p. 94, from a submission).
