Rules: write only `qa/tools/make_pack.py` and `qa/reports/pack_2026-11-07.md`; no git; no subagents; plain language; no time words; no em dashes. The pack is for Murray, who reads every Tier A line and samples Tier B. Build `make_pack.py` to write, from the register and defects: (1) a header with the date, the commit (`git rev-parse --short HEAD` is allowed read-only), and counts of claims by paper, tier and status; (2) **Tier A, one line per claim**: id, statement (shortened to one line), value, how produced (script and output file), the verifier reports that cover it (from `verifier_report`), status, and anything open (a defect id whose `what` names the row or its output file, or a status that is not `independently verified`); (3) the list of Tier A claims with fewer than two routes (status not `independently verified`, or a single report), which is what gate 2 cannot pass with; (4) **open items**: every defect whose `fixed_in` starts with "open" or "noted", grouped by layer; (5) a Tier B sample of 20 rows chosen by a fixed seed, same columns; (6) a one-paragraph "how to read this" for a reader who has not seen the registers. Run it with `--write` to produce `qa/reports/pack_2026-11-07.md`. Report: the counts, the Tier A lines that are not `independently verified` (ids and why), and the open-items count by layer.
