# Builder brief B15: classify the works that cite Murray's publications

Rules: write only `qa/reports/B15_citing_works.csv` and `qa/reports/B15_citing_works.md`; no git; no subagents; web fetch allowed for abstracts (publisher pages, Crossref, OpenAlex `https://api.openalex.org/works/<id>` or `works?filter=doi:<doi>&select=abstract_inverted_index,title`); never download a PDF into a repository; plain language; no time words.

Input: `qa/reports/cited_by_openalex_2026-11-07.md` (about 60 distinct citing works, with DOIs). For each distinct work (merge duplicates across the four lists), obtain the abstract (OpenAlex returns it as an inverted index; reconstruct it) and classify how it uses Murray's work: `applies` (uses the resource-based land-and-diet method or its results in its own analysis), `discusses` (compares or criticises the method or its findings), `passing` (cites it in a list of precedents or definitions), `unknown` (no abstract reachable). Also record: year, first author, country of the study area, the kind of carrying capacity it treats (food and land; water; urban or regional index; ecological; other), and whether the study area is Australia. Where the abstract does not settle the class, say `unknown`; do not guess from the title.

CSV columns: `doi, year, first_author, title, venue, study_country, capacity_kind, cites_which (2014 paper / 2015 paper / time-frames paper / thesis, any combination), class, basis (abstract / full text page / none)`. The md: counts by class and by capacity kind; the works classed `applies` or `discusses` with one line each on what they do; the Australian ones; anything surprising.
