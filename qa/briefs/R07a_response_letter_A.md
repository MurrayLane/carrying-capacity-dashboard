# Drafting brief R07a: the response letter to the second-round referees of Paper A

Rules: plain language; no em dashes anywhere; no time words; decision 33 tone (no verdicts on other authors; the referee is thanked once and answered point by point without flattery or concession beyond what the records support). No git; no subagents; no network. Write only `papers/A_results/response_letter_round2.md` and `qa/reports/R07a_response_letter.md`. Nothing is sent; this is a draft for Murray.

Inputs: the referee reports `qa/reports/R04a_rereview_A_demographer.md and R04b_rereview_A_land_systems.md`; the response plan `qa/reports/R05a_response_plan_A.md` (its blocks give, per issue, the verdict on the premise, the fair response and the response-letter words); the patch reports `qa/reports/B141a_patch_paper_A_round2.md (and B143a_patch_paper_A_to_V54a.md if it exists when you start)` (what the paper now says, section by section); `qa/DECISIONS.md` rows 134 to 140, 149, 150 and 133; the builder and checker reports the plan names for each issue (cite them by figure, never by job id in the letter itself); the current draft `papers/A_results/draft/paper_A_draft1.md` and supplement (quote the new sentence where a point was answered by a change).

Form: a letter to the editor (one paragraph: what changed in sum, with the three largest changes), then one section per referee, each issue in the referee's numbering, quoting the referee's point in one or two lines and answering in the form: what we did (with the section and the new sentence or figure), or what the records show where the premise is mistaken (with the figure and its source named in words), or why it is out of scope (with the sentence the paper now carries so the reader does not expect it). Where a standing decision rules a request out (decisions 33, 113, 118 as amended by 122 and 146, 139, 145), say so plainly as the author's choice with the reason. Where a figure moved, give old and new. End with a list of the works added to the references and the works the referee named that are not cited, with one clause each on why. No job ids, register ids, release codes or file paths anywhere in the letter (the report may carry them). Under 2,500 words.

Report: the issues answered by change, by rebuttal, by scope, with counts; every figure in the letter with its source report and checker; anything the letter promises that the paper does not yet carry (there should be none; if there is, say so); the word count; "Wrong-value check:" with one figure deliberately taken from a builder report where the checker amended it, caught against the checker, marked as an example.
