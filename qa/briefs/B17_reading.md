# Builder brief B17: read the priority literature into the register

Rules: write only `qa/reports/B17_reading.md` and update rows in `qa/literature_register.csv` (append rows for works not yet in it; keep CRLF and every column); no git; no subagents; web fetch allowed for abstracts and open-access full texts; never download a PDF into a repository; plain language; no time words. A paper is `read` only if you opened its full text (open access or a copy in `/home/user/carrying-capacity-models/Literature review/`); otherwise `abstract only`; say which in `read_or_reported`.

Read, in this order: (1) Khorsandi et al. 2022, "The edge of the petri dish for a nation: water resources carrying capacity assessment for Iran", Science of the Total Environment, 10.1016/j.scitotenv.2022.153038, and Khorsandi 2023 (Heliyon, 10.1016/j.heliyon.2023.e15079); (2) the ten works ranked in `qa/reports/B14_literature_sweep.md` (Hatfield-Dodds 2015 and the Australian National Outlook; Bryan 2016; Peters 2009 and 2022; Ridoutt 2017; Kastner 2012; Willett 2019 and Springmann 2018; Gerten 2020; Erb 2016; Giampietro and Saltelli 2014 with Blomqvist 2013 and the reply; Chapman and Byron 2018); (3) Sobels et al. 2010 if any copy is reachable (title "Long-term physical implications of net overseas migration: Australia in 2050").

For each: full citation with DOI; what it estimates or argues (three to five sentences, from the text you read); the method family in the methods paper's terms (resource-based land and diet; energy balance; commodity balance; footprint; land per diet; failure probability; nutrition optimisation; scenario model; index; concept review); the numbers it gives that bear on Australia or on a capacity range; how the methods paper should use it (catalogue, critique, comparator, context), in one line; and whether it cites or engages with Lane's work. Register row: `what_taken` one line, `used_by` "methods paper (section n)". Report: the list with the one-line uses, and anything that changes the paper's argument.
