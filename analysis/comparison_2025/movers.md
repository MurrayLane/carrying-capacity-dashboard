## Check of Swan and the arid movers (2026-11-03): several of these are land-definition effects, not change

`movers.csv` now has three flag columns: `land_only_ratio` (capacity with only the 2025 land layer over the 2011 capacity), `boundary_older_2011` (the five zones whose 2011 areas are on older boundaries) and `land_definition_effect_flag` (land alone moves capacity by more than 2 times or less than 0.5 times).

* **Swan (Perth) 170 to 6 is a boundary artefact, not a collapse.** The 2011 model's Swan is the region as drawn before the Perth boundary change: 13,536 km2 with 5,035 km2 of cropland; the 2011 NRM polygon, which the 2025 layers use, is 3,738 km2 with 29 km2 of cropland. Capacity follows land, and the land layer alone takes Swan from 193,162 to 2,413. The population is the whole Perth region in both (1.70 to 2.27 million), so over-capacity goes from 10 to 349 times. Swan is not usable as a 2011 to 2025 case; it is usable for 2025 alone. The same holds for the other zones on older boundaries: Desert Channels (2011 area 510,340 km2 against 435,435), SA Murray Darling Basin, Alinytjara Wilurara, Northern and Yorke (`analysis/land_2011/README.md`, point 1: the 2011 regions overcount their state rows by 74,905 km2 in Queensland, 27,920 in South Australia and 9,798 in Western Australia; the 2025 layers sum to the state rows within 2 km2).
* **SA Arid Lands (x13), Desert Channels (x7.7), Southern Gulf (x4.3) and Northern Gulf (x2.4) are mostly the land definition.** The land layer alone raises capacity 6.5, 5.7, 2.6 and 2.2 times (the median region is 0.90). These are rangeland zones whose capacity is a small residual after the reserve target and non-agricultural land, so a change in how reserve and non-agricultural land are measured (the 2011 reserve is an estimate that an IBRA-weighted version reproduces for only 7 of 53 zones; the 2025 reserve is measured on the land-use raster) moves capacity several-fold. Their populations also differ in definition (SA Arid Lands 31,361 in the 2011 model against 8,049 allocated from 2025 estimated resident population by residential land). The 2011 to 2025 change for these zones is a data-definition effect and should not be read as a real change in what the land can feed.
* **Hawkesbury-Nepean-Sydney 66 to 46 is usable with its flag stated**: the land layer alone gives 0.49, just inside the land-definition flag (below 0.5) (a smaller farm and pasture base on the same polygon, 389 to 191 km2 of cropland), no boundary change.
* **Suggested set, revised.** One per type without a boundary or definition flag: Hawkesbury-Nepean-Sydney (capital with hinterland, instead of Swan), Murray or Murrumbidgee-ACT, Avon, North Tasmania. For the arid type use a zone whose `land_only_ratio` is near 1 or show SA Arid Lands only with the definition caveat; the Eyre Peninsula pairing stands (no flag). Murray picks.
