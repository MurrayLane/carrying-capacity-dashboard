# Candidate regional cases for the results paper (Murray 2026-11-03: one region per type, from the largest movers; Murray picks)

Source: `movers.csv` (corrected method, `2011-fixed` against `2025-fixed`, the 52 NRM regions and the Northern Territory, short and long term; capacity, population, ratio and over-capacity flag at both dates). Thousands of people, short term.

| Type | Candidates (capacity 2011 to 2025; population 2011 to 2025; status) | Why it is a good case |
|---|---|---|
| Capital with hinterland | **Hawkesbury-Nepean-Sydney** 66 to 46 (pop 4,073 to 5,401; over capacity both); **Swan (Perth)** 170 to 6 (pop 1,699 to 2,269; over both); Port Phillip and Westernport 139 to 120; South East Queensland 175 to 183 | All stay over capacity; Swan's capacity collapses between the dates, which needs explaining before it is used (check the land layers: cropping 29 km2) |
| Murray-Darling Basin | **Murray** 752 to 905 (pop 102 to 121); **Murrumbidgee-ACT** 1,185 to 1,533 (pop 529 to 738); Lachlan 1,186 to 1,642; Namoi 875 to 1,317; Border Rivers-Gwydir 1,044 to 1,423 | The largest absolute gains in the country (Central West NSW +464, Lachlan +456, Namoi +441); all under capacity at both dates |
| South-west WA | **Avon** 971 to 1,080 (pop 55 to 69); South Coast WA 1,052 to 1,332 (+279); Northern Agricultural 416 to 522 | The cropping belt; under capacity throughout |
| Tasmania | **North Tasmania** 139 to 187 (pop 144 to 156): over capacity in 2011, under in 2025; North West Tasmania 77 to 98 (pop 114 to 120, over both); South Tasmania 52 to 52 (pop 248 to 299, over both) | One of the status flips |
| Arid | **SA Arid Lands** 2 to 28 (pop 31 to 8: over to under); **Rangelands (WA)** 111 to 167 (pop 161 to 157: over to under); Desert Channels over to under | The rangelands that gain capacity, and the mechanism case (stranded pasture) |
| Contrary mover | **Corangamite** under to over capacity | The only region that moves the wrong way in the short term |

Status flips on the corrected method, short term: Wet Tropics, Desert Channels, Mackay Whitsunday, Rangelands, North Tasmania, Southern Gulf, SA Arid Lands and North East Vic move from over to under capacity; Corangamite from under to over. Long term: Desert Channels, South West Qld, SA Arid Lands and Northern and Yorke move from over to under.

My suggested set (one per type, plus the mechanism pair): Hawkesbury-Nepean-Sydney or Swan (after checking Swan), Murray or Murrumbidgee-ACT, Avon, North Tasmania, SA Arid Lands with the Eyre Peninsula. The 2011 state populations were wrong (error 6), but the regional populations used here are the regions' own and are not affected.
