# Literature review since 2011 (methods paper, introduction and Part 2)

Murray, 2026-11-02: an updated review focusing on the papers published since 2011. Status: **plan and a verified seed list only; the search proper is not done.**

## Why it needs a scholarly index

The tools available to the assistant are a general web search (noisy, returns encyclopedia pages and news) and the Crossref metadata service (which verifies that a reference exists, with its DOI, but gives no citing papers and is rate-limited). The two citation indexes tried (OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar) refused requests. A review that is complete for 2011 to 2026 needs Scopus, Web of Science or Google Scholar, which Murray can reach and the assistant cannot.

## Proposed scope (to confirm)

1. **Papers that cite Murray's work** (Lane, Dawes and Grace 2014 *Ecological Modelling* 272; 2015 *Population and Environment* 36; Lane 2017 *Population and Environment* 38; the thesis): how the model is used and criticised.
2. **Resource-based and land-based carrying capacity**, national and regional (the Peters 2016 family, local food studies).
3. **Ecological footprint and biocapacity**, methods and critique since 2011 (national accounts, the Giampietro and Saltelli critique and the responses).
4. **Diet, land and nutrition**: land requirements of diets, optimisation, planetary boundaries and food (the nutrition-limited model's literature).
5. **Australian food capacity and food security studies**, 2011 to 2026.
6. **The carrying-capacity concept itself** (reviews of its definition and use).

Output: a register (this file) with each paper's reference, DOI, theme, one-line contribution **read from the paper**, and its use in the methods paper; then the review text.

## What Murray can export (specific routes)

* Google Scholar: open each of the four works above, click "Cited by", and save the results (the "Cite" or BibTeX export, or Scholar's "My library"); or Scopus or Web of Science at QUT's library: search the title for each and use "Cited by" then "Export" (RIS or CSV).
* Scopus or Web of Science keyword search 2011 to 2026 in titles, abstracts and keywords: `"carrying capacity" AND (food OR agricultur* OR land) AND (population OR diet)`; and `"ecological footprint" AND (national OR critique OR biocapacity)`.
* Save the exports in `Literature review/` of the models repository.
